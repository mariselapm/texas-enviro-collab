About this project

A prototype built by journalism students to connect the people covering Texas's environment: news organizations, creators, educators and reporters. We'd love your feedback.

How connecting works

Click Connect on anyone's card and answer a few short questions about you and your idea. The site writes the email for you: open it in Gmail or Outlook, or copy it into any email app, then review and send it from your own account. Nothing is stored on this site.

Want to be listed?

Everyone in the directory has agreed to be included. To join, or to add an event, email us at .

How the data is kept up to date

The whole site is powered by one spreadsheet (platform-data.xlsx) with a Contacts tab and an Events tab. When the team edits and saves it, the site shows the changes the next time it loads.
